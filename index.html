About Me

I'm passionate about solving complex problems through machine learning and software development. I love tackling challenging research problems and building innovative applications. My work spans from EMG data classification to gesture-controlled interfaces, combining my interests in AI and human-computer interaction. When I'm not coding or conducting research, I enjoy mentoring students and contributing to the tech community.
