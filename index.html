PUBLICATIONS

Reconstructing hand gestures with synergies extracted from dance movements

Parthan Olikkal, Chris Dollo, Akshara Ajendla, Ann Sofie Clemmensen & Ramana Vinjamuri

Scientific Reports • 2025

Comprehending and replicating human hand gestures is crucial for advancements in robotics, sign language interpretation, and human-computer interaction. This study introduces a novel approach to understanding hand gestures through structured movement primitives derived from mudras — hand gestures used in the Indian classical dance form Bharatanatyam. Hand gesture synergies were extracted using Gaussian-modeled joint angular velocities and represented as fundamental syllables of motion, then employed to reconstruct 75 diverse hand gestures including ASL postures, natural hand grasps, and traditional mudras. Mudra-derived synergies achieved 95.78% reconstruction accuracy for natural grasps and 92.99% for mudras, outperforming existing baseline models.
